# Big Bear 2026

A long weekend in the San Bernardino mountains — cabin, lake, hiking, and s'mores.

## Friday

**Arrival Day**

- Depart LA by noon to beat traffic
- Stop at Stater Bros on the way in for groceries
- Check in to the cabin at 3pm
- Unpack, explore the property
- Grill burgers and hot dogs for dinner
- Evening: bonfire and card games

## Saturday

**Lake Day**

- Lazy morning — pancakes and coffee on the deck
- Head to Big Bear Lake for kayaking and paddleboarding (rentals at Pine Knot Landing)
- Lunch: sandwiches at the lakefront
- Afternoon: swim and sunbathe
- Evening: pizza night + movie in the cabin

## Sunday

**Hike Day**

- Breakfast burritos
- Castle Rock Trail hike (2.5 miles, great views of the lake)
- Lunch in Big Bear Village — check out the shops
- Afternoon: relax at the cabin, board games
- Dinner: taco bar

## Monday

**Explore Day**

- Sleep in
- Alpine Slide at Magic Mountain for the adventurous ones
- Big Bear Discovery Center for the kids
- Dinner: BBQ ribs and corn on the cob

## Tuesday

**Chill Day**

- Morning walk around the lake
- Hammock time, reading, naps
- Afternoon: optional fishing at the lake
- Dinner: pasta night
- S'mores around the fire

## Wednesday

**Departure Day**

- Pack up after breakfast
- Clean the cabin
- Group photo before we leave!
- Stop in Redlands for lunch on the way home
